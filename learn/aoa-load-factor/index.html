Before you play

Angle of attack is chord line vs relative wind. A stall happens when you exceed the critical AOA—at any airspeed. In turns and pull-ups, load factor rises, so the airspeed at which you hit that same critical AOA goes up: \(V_s = V_{s1}\sqrt{n}\).

  1. Geometry — see AOA, not pitch to the horizon
  2. CL curve — raise AOA to the critical angle and stall
  3. Load factor — bank angle → Gs → higher stall speed
  4. Scenarios — level, steep turn, accelerated stall
  5. Guide — story + quiz
