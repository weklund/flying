# Private Pilot Ground School — Study Index

**Private Pilot Ground School** · MIT **16.687** · January IAP 2019

**Instructors:** Dr. Philip Greenspun, Dr. Tina Srivastava

This is a three-day workshop that took place during the MIT Independent Activities Period (IAP) in January, 2019. This workshop aims to provide information for students to prepare for the FAA Private Pilot Knowledge Test. Topics include airplane aerodynamics, aircraft systems, navigation, meteorology, aircraft ownership and maintenance, aircraft performance, multi-engine and jets.

**License:** CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (MIT OCW materials)

**Offline course root:** [`16.687-january-iap-2019/index.html`](16.687-january-iap-2019/index.html)

---

## Quick start

1. Open the [study home](index.html) (or the GitHub Pages URL after deploy).
2. Explore density altitude & performance with the [Aircraft Performance Lab](learn/aircraft-performance/index.html) (educational only).
3. Read short [study notes](notes/) and drill with the [quiz](quiz/index.html).
4. Work MIT sessions below (local OCW dump or [OCW online](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/16-687-private-pilot-ground-school-january-iap-2019/)).
5. FAA handbooks: [official PHAK / AFH](https://www.faa.gov/regulations_policies/handbooks_manuals) or `python3 scripts/download_faa_handbooks.py` (PDFs not in git).
6. Optional: `python3 scripts/download_videos.py` to fetch lecture MP4s from archive.org.
